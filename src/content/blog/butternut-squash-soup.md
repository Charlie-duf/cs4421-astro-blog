This is definetely one of my favourite foods for a cold day. The chilies in this dish help warm you up but they don't overpower the squashes flavours making for a suprisingly filling dish.
<br>
This warm and filling soup pairs perfectly with yesterdays soda bread.
<br>
*Every ingredient tells a story of the earth's bounty.*

## Ingredients

- 1 Butternut squash (about 1kg, peeled and deseeded)
- 2 onions, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 mild red chilies, deseeded and finely chopped
- 850ml hot vegetable stock
- 4 tbsp creme fraiche (and more to serve)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 180*C
2. Cut the squash into large cubes, about 4cm across and toss into a large tray/tin with 1 tbsp olive oil
3. Roast for 30 minutes, turning once, until golden and soft.
4. Meanwhile, melt the butter with the remaining olive oil in a large saucepan. Add the onions, garlic, and 3/4 of the chopped chilies.
5. Cook and cover on a low heat for 15-20 minutes until the onions are completely soft.
6. Add the squash to the pan alongside the stock and creme fraiche.
7. Liquidise, season to taste and serve with creme fraiche and remaining chilies.

### Tips

This soup goes incredibly well with a thick bread slice and some salted butter. It can be served as a full dinner but I'd recommend keeping this for lunchtime.
