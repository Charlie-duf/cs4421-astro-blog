Literally 'Pasta with four types of tomatoes', this recipe was sent to me by a family friend and it is incredibly simple and delicious. Through this recipe I discovered a love for the umami flavour (and also what umami was).
<br>
The mix of dried tomatoes and puree (two of my favourite ingredients) make this one of my go to comfort foods whenever I'm hungry.


## Ingredients

- 200g cherry tomatoes, sliced in two
- 4 garlic cloves
- 4 sundried tomatoes, chopped
- 2 tbsp of tomato puree
- 400g of canned plum tomatoes
- Handful of fresh basil, chopped
- Olive oil
- Pasta

## Method

1. Crush the plum tomatoes in a bowl with your hands (there should still be large enough chunks of tomato).
2. In a large pan heat up about 5 tbsp olive oil. Fry the garlic for a couple minutes in the hot oil.
3. Add the sliced cherry tomatoes into the oil and garlic.
4. Let cook for 3 minutes before adding in the crushed plum tomatoes, chopped sundried tomatoes, tomato puree, basil, and a bit of salt. Mix.
5. Start cooking the pasta (however much you like).
6. Let cook until most the water has evaporated and the mixture has thickened.
7. Find all the garlic cloves and crush them before mixing them back into the mixture.
8. Drain the pasta and mix into the tomato mix.


### Tips
I recommend taking a couple table spoons of the pasta water and mixing it into the mixture for a starchier sauce.
<br>
Serve with parmesean
<br>
You can add bacon bits or spinach to this recipe if you'd like (just add them after the cherry tomatoes)
