Every St Patrick's day my family meet up at one of our houses and have a family dinner. Each time I try to bring some kind of traditional Irish food. This year I brought this delicious soda bread, the recipe of which I learned on a school trip to Causey Farm. 
<br>
*A recipe has no soul. You as the cook, must bring soul to the recipe*

## Ingredients

- 250g wholemeal flour
- 200g white flour
- 1tsp baking soda, sieved
- 1 tsp salt 
- 1 egg
- 350ml buttermilk
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds (optional)
- 1 tbsp porridge oats (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 180*C, line a baking tin with parchment paper.
2. Mix all the dry ingredients in a bowl (other than the seeds or oats, if using).
3. In another bowl, mix the wet ingredients until well combined.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix into a soft dough.
5. Add the dough to the tin, spread evenly. Sprinkly the oats/seeds on top if using.
6. Put the sign of the cross on the dough (to keep the devil out, or to allow even cooking and expansion).
7. Put in oven for 45-50 minutes.
8. When cooked, take out of the tin and wrap in a clean tea towel to keep the crust soft.

### Tips

Cut thick slices and butter well. Goes well with soups.
