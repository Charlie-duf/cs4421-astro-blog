My grandparents gifted my Avoca's at home cookbook for my birthday and this was one of my favourites. Both the cookies and the filling keep for a long time so I usually make big batches and freeze them.
It can be difficult to find quality hazelnut butter in most supermarkets, but its well worth it. If necessary you can make this recipe with almond or peanut butter.

## Ingredients

### Cookies

- 225g soft butter
- 225 caster sugar
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp baking soda (bicarbonate)
- 250g plain flour
- 250g chocolate chips

### Filling

- 100ml cream
- 250g marscarpone cheese
- 100g hazelnut butter
- 120g icing sugar

## Method

### Cookies

1. Preheat oven to 170*C
2. Beat sugar and butter together until creamy, then mix in the yolk and vanilla
3. In another bowl mix the flour and baking soda together, add into the butter/sugar mix. Combine with a wooden spoon before adding the chocolate chips.
4. Line a tray with parchment paper, scoop small spoons of cookie dough, put them on the tray, leaving gaps between scoops.
5. Bake in oven for 10-12 minutes, until golden. Transfer to a wire rack to cool.

### Filling

1. Whip cream until soft peaks form.
2. In another bowl mix all the other ingredients. Then gently fold it into the cream bit-by-bit.

### Tips
Take two cookies and a spoonful of the filling, spread the filling on the bottom side of one cookie and press the other cookie into it.
<br>
Crush some whole hazelnuts and sprinkle it into the filling for some extra crunch
