## Smoky Beef & Pepper Tacos

These tacos are bold, savory, and perfect for a busy weeknight. Crisp peppers and juicy beef come together quickly with warm spices and a squeeze of lime.

### Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon oregano
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce
- 8 small tortillas
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Lime wedges and cilantro, for serving

### Instructions

1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the ground beef until browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.
2. Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until softened.
3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper.
4. Add the tomato sauce and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until the mixture thickens.
5. Warm the tortillas and spoon in the beef filling.
6. Top with cilantro and a squeeze of lime before serving.

### Tips

For an easy upgrade, add shredded cabbage and pickled jalapeños. They add crunch and a bright contrast to the rich beef.
