For a family of incredibly talented cooks, we don't have really have any family recipes to be passed down, this meatball recipe is the only exception. My grandmother used to cook this recipe for her children and when I was younger. She gave this recipe to me.
<br>
I'm a big fan of hefty meals and this one is probably the best recipe you'll find on this site.

## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- 675g lean minced beef
- 450g well flavoured sausage meat (both traditional and italian herb work well)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped (or blitzed)
- 1/2 tsp mixed herbs
- 1/4 rosemary needles
- 3 tbsp chopped parsley
- 1 tsp powdered garlic
- 2 eggs
- 125g bread crumbs
- 5-6 tbsp oil (any, really)

### Sauce 

- 1.6kg canned tomatoes (4 400g cans)
- 10-12 tbsp tomato puree
- 2 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp powdered garlic
- 1 tsp ginger (ground)
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 3-4 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 pint red wine (non-alcoholic is fine)
- 4 tbsp cornflour
- 8 tbsp cold water


## Instructions

### Meatballs

1. Mix all the ingredients into a bowl. Beat until well mixed (you can use a wooden spoon but I find that mixing with your hands is the most efficient).
2. Shape the mixture into small-medium sized balls.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the meatballs for at least 5 minutes on each side (don't worry about them being undercooked as they'll cook more later on).

### Sauce

1. Preheat oven to 180*C
2. Place all the ingredients except the cornflour and water in a large saucepan (you'll need a very big saucepan for this, you can also use two smaller ones if necessary).
3. Bring to the boil, stirring all the time. Simmer for 10 minutes
4. Liquidise the mixture and bring back to the boil. Taste and adjust flavour and consitency (it will thicken in the oven).
5. Mix the cornflour and water together and mix into the sauce.
6. Add the cooked meatballs into the saucepan (ensure they are fully covered). Put in the oven for 30 mins.
7. Meanwhile prepare the spaghetti.

### Tips

If you have the time, I recommend leaving this in the fridge overnight to get the best flavour before heating it back up (on the stove).
<br>
This recipe makes a lot of portions (I served this to a group of 15 once and everybody had at least two bowls) so make sure you plan accordingly.
