## Tuscan White Bean & Kale Soup

This hearty soup is cozy, nourishing, and full of savory depth. White beans and kale simmer in a tomato broth with garlic and herbs until everything tastes deeply satisfying.

### Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 2 cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 2 cups chopped kale
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Optional: lemon zest and crusty bread for serving

### Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and celery and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
2. Stir in the garlic, thyme, and rosemary and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes, beans, and vegetable broth. Bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
4. Use a potato masher to lightly crush some of the beans for a thicker texture.
5. Stir in the kale and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until wilted.
6. Season with salt and pepper, then serve with lemon zest and crusty bread if desired.

### Tips

A splash of white wine adds extra depth if you have it on hand. This soup also reheats beautifully for lunch the next day.
