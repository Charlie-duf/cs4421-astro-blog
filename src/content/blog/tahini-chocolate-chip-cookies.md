Tahini adds a subtle toasted flavor to these easy drop cookies and keeps the centers soft. No mixer is needed: a bowl, a whisk, and a short chill are all it takes.

**Makes:** 16 cookies | **Prep:** 15 minutes, plus 30 minutes chilling | **Bake:** 10-12 minutes per tray

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon fine salt
- ½ cup (115 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- ½ cup (120 g) well-stirred tahini
- ¾ cup (150 g) packed light brown sugar
- ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup (170 g) dark chocolate chips or chopped chocolate
- Flaky salt, optional

## Method

1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, and fine salt together in a medium bowl.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the melted butter, tahini, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth. Whisk in the egg and vanilla.
3. Add the dry ingredients and stir just until no streaks of flour remain. Fold in the chocolate.
4. Cover and chill the dough for 30 minutes. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment.
5. Scoop the dough into 2-tablespoon portions, spacing them about 2 inches (5 cm) apart. Bake one tray at a time for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are set and lightly golden but the centers still look soft.
6. Sprinkle with flaky salt, if using. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes before moving them to a rack.

**Storage:** Keep the cooled cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days.
