My godmother sent this recipe to me when I was sick, its a south asian spice mix to reduce inflammation, help digestion, and aid sleep. I always have a bit of the mixture available in a small jar for whenever I need it.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup ground tumeric 
- 2 tbsp ground ginger
- 1 and 1/2 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp ground star anise
- Minch ground black peppers
- Maple syrup / honey (optional)

## Instructions

1. Combine the spices in a clean glass jar (not the maple syrup or honey)
2. Warm milk in a saucepan
3. Add 1-2 tsp of the mix into a mug and combine well with however much syrup/honey you want.
4. When combined pour the hot milk into the mug, mix well and drink.

### Tips

This keeps for a month or two when stored in a cool dry place away from direct sunlight.
