My mother came up with this recipe as a way to use up some prawns and it became a classic dish in my household. My only complaint with this dish is that theres never enough for seconds.


## Ingredients

- 2 bell peppers
- 400g prawns
- 2 lemons
- 8 garlic cloves (medium-big)
- 2 fresh chilies
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Bowl of fresh parsley
- Rice / noodles (precooked)
- Sliced cherry tomatoes (optional)

## Instructions

1. Chop bell peppers into small squares, steam 10 minutes until slightly soft.
2. Mash the garlic into a large pan/wok on medium heat with olive oil and the chilies (finely chopped).
3. After a minute add the tomato (if using) and the smoked paprika.
4. Juice lemons and add it to the mix. After a minute add the peppers and prawns.
5. After another minute add precooked rice or noodles.
6. Once the prawns are cooked take the pan off the heat and add the parsley.

### Tips

If using noodles I recommend lowering the total amount of lemon juice as there's less absorption going on.
