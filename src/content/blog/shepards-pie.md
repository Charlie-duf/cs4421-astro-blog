This is another one of the best winter foods in my opinion. It's incredibly filling and makes for a delicious dinner when its cold and dreary outside.
<br>
I got the base recipe from a Jamie Oliver cookbook but I've since modified it to meet my own tastes. This recipe is perfect for using up old vegetables.
<br>
*Even vegetable scraps have their uses*

## Ingredients

- 500g lean minced lamb
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 400g tin cannelini beans
- 2 onions
- 2 carrots
- 2 sticks of celery
- 250g chestnut mushrooms
- 2 heaped tbsp plain flour
- 800ml chicken/veg stock
- 2 tsp powdered garlic
- 1kg potatoes, peeled
- 2tbsp milk
- 15g cheddar cheese
- 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 350g frozen peas
- Powdered chili and paprika (optional)

## Instructions

1. Put the mince into a cold casserole pan, place on high heat. Add a good pinch of black pepper and cook for 15 minutes or until golden brown, mixing well.
2. Finely chop the rosemary and drain (but don't rinse) the cannelini beans. Stir both in and cook for another 10 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, finely chop the celery, mushrooms, onions and carrots. Mix them into the pan and sweat for 10 minutes on medium high.
4. Stir in the flour, wait until the mixture starts to become thick and gooey before adding in the stock. Add salt and powdered garlic.
5. Bring to the boil, add in the chili/paprika (if using), and simmer on a low heat with the lid on for 30 minutes.
6. Preheat the oven to 180*C
7. While the vegetables are cooking cut the potatoes into 3cm chunks and cook them in a large pan of water until mushy.
8. Drain the potatoes well, then mash them with the milk and cheese. Add salt and pepper if you wish.
9. Check the consistency of the mince, you want it slightly wetter than you'd think as it'll thicken further in the oven.
10. Add worcestershire sauce.
11. In a large glass tray spread the peas evenly on the bottom. Using a ladle add spoonfuls of mince mix on top until the peas are covered. Put the potato mash on top of the mince, covering as much of it as possible.
12. Using a fork, make hills and valleys in the potato mix so that it will turn golden and crispy. Bake it for 50 minutes. 

### Tips

Prepare this meal the night before and let the flavours marinate before reheating it in the oven.
<br>
Serve on hot plates.
