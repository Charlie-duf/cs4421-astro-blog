Roasting tomatoes concentrates their sweetness and creates a no-fuss sauce right in the baking dish. A little pasta water brings everything together, while cool spoonfuls of ricotta make each bowl creamy.

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (700 g) cherry tomatoes
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes, optional
- ¾ teaspoon fine salt, plus more for the pasta water
- 12 oz (340 g) short pasta, such as rigatoni or fusilli
- ½ cup (120 g) whole-milk ricotta
- ½ cup (about 15 g) torn fresh basil leaves
- ⅓ cup (35 g) finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Put the tomatoes and garlic in a baking dish. Drizzle with olive oil and add the red pepper flakes, salt, and a few grinds of black pepper. Toss to coat.
2. Roast for 20–25 minutes, until the tomatoes have burst and their juices are bubbling.
3. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until just al dente, following the package timing. Reserve 1 cup (240 ml) of pasta water, then drain.
4. Lightly mash some of the roasted tomatoes in the dish with a spoon. Add the drained pasta and Parmesan, tossing to coat. Add pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce clings to the pasta.
5. Fold in half of the basil. Divide between bowls and finish with ricotta, the remaining basil, and extra Parmesan.

**Make it ahead:** Roast the tomatoes up to 2 days in advance and refrigerate them. Reheat gently while the pasta cooks.
