I learned this recipe in home economics and I've made it at least once every 3 months since
<br>
I love this recipe because its so versitile, you can serve it for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or dessert by only modifying the filling in the crepe.
This is the simplest recipe you'll find on this site. Its so simple that I know it off by heart.


## Ingredients

- 100g plain flour
- 2 eggs
- 300ml milk

## Instructions

1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl.
2. Set in a fridge for 30 minutes to settle (optional)
3. Wipe oil / butter in a small-medium pan and put on high heat.
4. When hot, add a ladle of batter to the pan, spread it around until it covers the whole pan.
5. Let cook until golden brown on the underside, then take a spatula and flip it over to cook for a minute or two on the other side.
6. Repeat until batter is finished.

### Ideas for fillings

#### Savory crepes


<ul> 
<li>Ham and cheddar cheese</li>
<li>Ham, relish, and mozzarella </li>
<li>Bacon and eggs </li>
<li>Tomato, onion and goats cheese </li>
</ul>
<br>
I trick I learned was to start cooking the savory ingredients while the crepe itself is still cooking (cook one side of the crepe, flip and place the ingredients on the cooked side while the underside is still cooking, then fold the crepe and serve).

#### Dessert crepes
<ul>
<li> Chocolate and raspberries </li>
<li> Small bit of salted butter and bananas</li>
<li> Caster sugar and lemon juice</li>
<li> Maple syrup and blueberries</li>
</ul>
