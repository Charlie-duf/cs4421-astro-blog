## Spinach & Feta Breakfast Burrito

This breakfast burrito is quick, satisfying, and full of flavor. It brings together scrambled eggs, sautéed greens, black beans, and melty feta in a warm tortilla.

### Ingredients

- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup fresh spinach, chopped
- 2 tablespoons crumbled feta
- 1 small flour tortilla
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/4 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Optional: salsa, avocado, and hot sauce

### Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the spinach and cook for 30 seconds until just wilted.
2. Stir in the black beans, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until warmed through.
3. Push the vegetables to one side of the skillet and scramble the eggs in the open space until softly set.
4. Fold everything together and sprinkle with feta.
5. Spoon the filling into a warm tortilla, then fold in the sides and roll tightly.
6. Serve with salsa, avocado, or hot sauce if desired.

### Tips

If you want extra texture, toast the burrito in a dry skillet for a minute on each side. It gives the outside a crisp finish without losing the soft center.
