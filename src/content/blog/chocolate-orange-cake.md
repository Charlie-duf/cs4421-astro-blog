This recipe was given to me by my godmother and it is one of my favourite meals to bring as a housewarming gift. The recipe itself is incredibly simple to follow and it makes one of, if not the moistest, richest cake I've ever had. Put this cake in the refrigerator for an hour before serving to get the best results.

### Ingredients

- 1 large orange (or two medium sized oranges)
- 250g caster sugar 
- 200g ground almonds
- 50g cocoa powder
- 6 eggs
- 1 heaped tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda

### Instructions

1. Boil the orange(s) for 2hrs or until soft.
2. Preheat oven to 180*C
3. Blend the oranges into a pulp, add to a bowl alongside all other ingredients
4. Mix together with a whisk until everything is combined into a slightly grainy chocolate coloured mixture.
5. Line a baking tin with parchment paper, add mixture in evenly and place in oven for 50 minutes or until a knife passes through cleanly.

### Tips

This cake is best served chilled with vanilla icecream and fresh raspberries.
<br>
I recommend preparing the orages ahead of time so that you don't have to dedicate an extra 2 hrs to this recipe
