Bone-in chicken thighs stay juicy while the potatoes roast underneath and soak up all the lemony pan juices. A sturdy roasting pan means less washing up and an easy dinner to share.

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep:** 15 minutes | **Cook:** 50-60 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (700 g) baby potatoes, halved
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 teaspoon fine salt, divided
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lb / 900 g total)
- 1 lemon, thinly sliced, plus 1 tablespoon juice
- 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh rosemary, or 1 teaspoon dried
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ cup (120 ml) chicken stock

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the potatoes and onion in a roasting pan with 1 tablespoon olive oil, half the salt, and the pepper. Spread into an even layer.
2. Pat the chicken dry. Rub it with the remaining oil, salt, rosemary, and oregano. Arrange the chicken skin-side up over the vegetables.
3. Tuck the garlic and lemon slices around the pan, then pour the stock into the pan without pouring it over the chicken skin.
4. Roast for 45–55 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the thickest part of each thigh reaches 165°F (74°C) on an instant-read thermometer. For extra-crisp skin, broil for 1–2 minutes at the end, watching closely.
5. Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes. Stir the lemon juice into the pan juices and spoon them over the chicken and potatoes to serve.

**If the potatoes are large:** Cut them into roughly 1-inch (2.5 cm) pieces so they cook through at the same time as the chicken.
