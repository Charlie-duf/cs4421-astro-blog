Need uses for yesterday's syrup? Check out this recipe!
<br>
Jelly is definitely one of my favourite desserts, serve this recipe with a proper vanilla icecream and whole whipped cream and you'll see why I love it so much!
<br>
*Eat what makes your soul happy!*

## Ingredients 

- 6 gelatine leaves
- 1 litre fruit syrup (cool or cold)

## Method

1. Soak the gelatine leaves in cold water for 5 minutes.
2. Heat around 100ml syrup in a small pan until hot.
3. Remove the gelatine leaves from the water and add to the hot syrup. Stir until the gelatine is dissolved.
4. In a bowl, combine the gelatine mixture and remaining syrup.
5. Put it in the fridge overnight to cool and set.

### Tips

Serve with vanilla ice cream and whipped cream.
