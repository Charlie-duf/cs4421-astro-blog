When I was a child, our family gathered at my grandparent's house for Christmas dinner, my grandmother has since gotten too old to prepare the whole thing by herself so my uncle and I have stepped in recent years. This recipe is a family favourite because it gives the crispiest roast potatoes I have ever had.


## Ingredients 

- 2kg potatoes
- 1/2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 5 tbsp oil (or animal fat)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- A handful of rosemary, chopped
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200*C
2. Peel and chop the potatoes into chunks about 4 cm across.
3. Boil the potatoes in a large pot with 2 tbsp salt and the bicarbonate of soda until a knife meets little resistance when inserted into a potato chunk.
4. Combine the oil/fat with the rosemary, garlic and pepper in a small saucepan and cook for 3-4 minutes over medium heat.
5. Strain the infused oil through a strainer. Set the oil and solid bits aside (in two different bowls.
6. When the potatoes are done, drain them and leave them for another minute to allow extra moisture evaporation.
7. Transfer the potatoes to a large bowl with the oil at the bottom. Season and toss to coat, until a thick layer of mashed potato paste has built up on the chunks.
8. Line a baking tray with parchment paper and spread the potatoes out on it. 9. Put in the oven without moving for 20 minutes. Then shake and flip the potatoes. Continue roasting for another 30-40 minutes.
10. Mix the garlic/rosemary mixture with the roast potatoes and serve.

### Tips

Prepare the potatoes ahead of time so that all you need to do is pop them in the oven
