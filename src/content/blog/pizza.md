I learnt this recipe during my first year in home economics and have tried to make it at least once a year, overall I find it's much cheaper than ordering a pizza and usually fresher and tastier too!


## Ingredients 

- 450g strong white flour / bread flour 
- 8g yeast
- 5 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp caster sugar
- Salt and herbs (whichever you like)
- Water
- At least 150ml tomato pasata (can use more)
- 75g shredded mozzarella 
- Toppings (precooked)

## Method 

1. Place 450g flour into a large bowl and add 2-3 pinches of salt 
2. Add 300ml cold water to a jug and mix the yeast into it.
3. Boil 250ml of water and mix said boiling water with the sugar. Pour into the cold yeast water.
4. Pour some of the mixture into the flour and mix with a wooden spoon, keep pouring and mixing. Make sure to keep a doughy consistency (you don't have to use all the liquid).
5. Remove the dough and place onto a floured board or counter.
6. Knead for 5-10 minutes, afterwards it should be properly floured (though the dough should be a little bit sticky).
7. Grease a bowl with the olive oil. Make sure it is greased properly to prevent the dough from sticking.
8. Place the dough in the bowl and cover with a (clean) damp tea towel. Let rest for around an hour and 20 minutes on your counter.
9. Preheat the oven to 250*C
10. Place the dough onto a floured counter / board and kneed until no longer sticky (avoid taking out the olive oil).
11. Cut the dough into 4 equal sized pieces. Flour a rolling pin and flatten one piece of dough into a thin circle.
12. Place the flat dough on a floured baking tray. Mix the herbs and pasata together and spread it evenly around the dough. Sprinkle the mozzarella on top and add your desired toppings.
13. Repeat with the other 3 pieces of dough
14. Place the tray in the oven for 15 minutes.

### Tips

Pizza topping suggestions:
* Ham, corn, and pineapple 
* Chicken, peppers and BBQ sauce (instead of pasata)
* Tomatoes, olives, and anchovies.
<br>
Make sure to use an oven safe tray, once I put a wooden board in there and the pizza stained it.
