This sauce is an Easter classic and goes perfectly with roast lamb or rabbit, I make a large batch of this in preparation for our family's Easter lunch. We have a couple mint plants that grow in our front lawn so sourcing the fresh mint is incredibly easy.
<br>
*One cannot think well, love well, sleep well, if one has not dined well.*

## Ingredients 

- 30g mint
- 2 tbsp caster sugar 
- 2 tbsp malt vinegar 

## Method

1. Wash and finely chop the mint.
2. Place in a bowl and cover with boiling water. Leave to steep for 3-4 minutes before straining out the water.
3. Rinse the leaves with cold water. Mix them with the sugar, vinegar, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl.
4. Refrigerate for 20-30 minutes before serving.

 
### Tips

Serve with roast lamb or rabbit (I reccomend spreading it on the fatty side of the meat to allow the vinegar taste to cut through)
