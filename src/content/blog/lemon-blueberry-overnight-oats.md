These overnight oats take a few minutes to stir together the night before and are ready to eat straight from the fridge in the morning. Lemon zest keeps them fresh and fragrant, while chia seeds help make the texture thick and creamy.

**Makes:** 2 servings | **Prep:** 10 minutes | **Chill:** at least 4 hours

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (90 g) rolled oats
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1 cup (240 ml) milk of choice
- ½ cup (120 g) plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup or honey
- Finely grated zest of 1 lemon
- 1 cup (150 g) blueberries, fresh or frozen
- Pinch of salt
- To serve: extra blueberries, chopped almonds, or a spoonful of yogurt

## Method

1. Stir the oats, chia seeds, milk, yogurt, maple syrup, lemon zest, and salt together in a bowl or lidded container.
2. Fold in the blueberries. If using frozen berries, there is no need to thaw them first.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
4. Stir before serving. Add a splash of milk if the oats are thicker than you like, then finish with your choice of toppings.

**Make it yours:** Swap the blueberries for chopped strawberries, or use a dairy-free yogurt and milk for a plant-based version.
