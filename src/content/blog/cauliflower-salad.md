Another one of my mother's recipes. This one makes for a very healthy yet delicous side salad. I'm not really a fan of cauliflower but this dish is still tasty.
<br>
*When the world wearies, and society ceases to satisfy, there is always the garden.*

## Ingredients 

### Salad

- Head of cauliflower 
- 120g shelled pistachios
- 2 X 400g tin chickpeas
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 3 tbsp chopped coriander 
- 2 tbsp maple syrup 
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 tbsp ground tumeric 
- 1 tbsp curry powder 
- 1 garlic clove, minced 

### Yogurt dressing

- 2 tbsp coriander seeds
- 2 tbsp cumin seeds
- Juice of 1 lime
- 6 tbsp Greek yogurt 

## Method

### Salad

1. Preheat oven to 190*C
2. Chop the cauliflower into bite-sized florets.
3. Mix garlic, curry powder, tumeric, fennel seeds, and some salt and pepper with the olive oil and maple syrup.
4. Spread this mix evenly over the cauliflower florets.
5. Line a baking tray with parchment paper. Spread the cauliflower on the tray. Place in the oven for 10 minutes or until a knife passes through with a little bit of resistance.
6. Reduce the oven temperature to 160*C, scatter the pistachios over the tray and toast in oven for 5-10 minutes.
7. Let cool, then transfer to a large bowl, add chickpeas, coriander, and toss to combine. Add lemon juice, a splash of olive oil, salt and pepper then mix again.

### Dressing 

1. Toast the coriander and cumin seeds in a hot dry frying pan over a high heat for 2-3 minutes.
2. Cool on a plate, then grind. Add the mix to the yoghurt along with the lime juice. Whisk to combine.

### Tips 

If necessary you can replace the coriander and cumin seeds in the dressing with their grounded version - just mix everything together.
