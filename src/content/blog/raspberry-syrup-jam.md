I got this recipe from the Ballymaloe dessert book and its well worth making this from scratch rather than buying premade. This recipe makes a tasty raspberry syrup as well as a lot of jam for toast.

## Ingredients 

- 500g raspberries (fresh or frozen)
- 250ml water
- 250g caster sugar


## Method

1. Mix all the ingredients into a large saucepan or pot and boil until the raspberries are mushy (falling apart).
2. Use a sieve to separate the syrup (liquid) and jam (solid bits).
3. Let both cool before using.

### Tips

Given the amount of raspberries needed I recommend using frozen raspberries to keep cost down.
<br>
This recipe can also be used to make other syrup and jams (just substitute according)
