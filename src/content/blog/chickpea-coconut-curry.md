This pantry-friendly curry is rich enough to feel cozy but quick enough for a weeknight. The chickpeas simmer in a warmly spiced coconut sauce, and a handful of spinach goes in at the end for a fresh finish.

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep:** 10 minutes | **Cook:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon freshly grated ginger
- 1 tablespoon curry powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (14 oz / 400 ml) coconut milk
- 2 cans (15 oz / 400 g each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- ½ teaspoon fine salt, plus more to taste
- 3 packed cups (90 g) baby spinach
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Cooked rice and chopped cilantro, to serve

## Method

1. Warm the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and beginning to turn golden.
2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 30 seconds. Stir in the curry powder, cumin, turmeric, and tomato paste; cook for another minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and coconut milk. Add the chickpeas and salt, then stir well.
4. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 15–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
5. Stir in the spinach until wilted, then add the lime juice. Taste and adjust the salt.
6. Serve over rice with cilantro on top.

**Serving idea:** Warm flatbread is great for scooping up the extra sauce.
